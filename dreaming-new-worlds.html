Collective project · 2024

Totem

A collaborative installation by Chronoverse, presented in Kedu Lagos, the 2024 exhibition concluding the second edition of Dreaming New Worlds.

Group / collaborative work

Chronoverse comprised Olúbùnmi Atere, Olorunyemi Kolapo Obadiah and Isaac Oloche Johnson. The collective developed Totem around rites of passage, the Igbo apprenticeship system (Igba boi), and the material representation of totems across cultures.

Kedu Lagos was curated by Chinyere Obieze and presented at the Old Government Printing Press in Lagos as the concluding exhibition of Dreaming New Worlds 2024.

Verified context

Goethe-Institut describes Dreaming New Worlds 2024 as a programme bringing science-based art projects into collaborative development toward two installations and an exhibition. The final exhibition, Kedu Lagos, addressed the contextualisation of African collective wisdom.

TechCabal and Art Report independently identify Totem as a Chronoverse installation exploring rites of passage, Igba boi and communal traditions.
