Biography

Isaac “Ollie” Oloche Johnson (b. 2001, United Kingdom) is a British-Nigerian artist, cultural researcher, creative coder and painter based in Aberdeen, Scotland. His practice works across code-based installation, digital print, painting and research, examining land, memory and extractivism in Nigeria and the wider region.

Johnson’s work draws on Igbo philosophies of nature and cultural memory while considering how technology can become a material and research tool. His practice includes digital art, oil painting, mixed-media installation and interactive work.

He was a 2024 Dreaming New Worlds Fellow at the Goethe-Institut Nigeria and a 2025 Artist in Residence at GroundWork Gallery, Norfolk, where he developed the Fluid Earth series.
