The
Traveller’s
Archive

An evolving transnational archive connecting audiovisual collections in Switzerland and Nigeria to reconsider movement, memory and Swiss–Nigerian encounters.

Work in progress

The project began with a discovery: grainy handheld film reels shot by Swiss traveller Hans Hürzeler during journeys across Nigeria and beyond in the 1940s and 1950s. The material moves through Lagos, eastern Nigeria and a weeks-long journey by boat from Nigeria toward Switzerland via Morocco and Portugal.

The project combines artistic research, digital humanities and archival practice. Its current development is centred on provenance, rights, metadata, annotation and relationships between Swiss institutions, family archives and future Nigerian partners.

Development

2025 — foundation

The project dossier describes 2025 as a foundation year focused on Swiss archival research, topic identification, annotation testing, metadata principles and the beginning of family-memory work.

2026 — consolidation

The current plan centres on stabilising archival access, rights and technical standards, refining provenance and consent pathways, working with the Digital Humanities Lab, and developing the project’s film and public research structures.

2027 — activation

The dossier proposes a wider phase of partnerships with Nigerian institutions and family-held materials, alongside public-facing film and exhibition work.

By Boat, By Memory

The planned public expression of the archive is conceived as both film and exhibition, grounded in archival film, personal memory, contemporary encounters and the long afterlife of Swiss–Nigerian relations.
